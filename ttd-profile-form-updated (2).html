General Instructions for All Sevaks
General Sevak(Tirumala/Tirupati)
Navaneetha Seva

Login Srivari Seva https://ttdevasthanams.ap.gov.in/

• Click on Log In

• Enter mobile Number

• Enter OTP which is sent to the above mobile number

• After successful login Click on Srivari Seva in menu

• Read the Instructions carefully

• Click on the Checkbox

I here by declare that I will follow instructions, terms and conditions of Srivari Seva

• Choose Srivari Seva Type by Clicking the radio (round) button

• Individual Registration

• Select Date of Seva from the Calendar

• Select Number of Sevaks (Minimum 10, Maximum 15)

• Click on Continue to proceed to registration form

• Fill in all required details for each Sevak member

• Click Save and Add Sevak after each member's details

• After adding all members, click Continue to review and confirm booking
